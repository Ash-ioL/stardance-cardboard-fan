# My Cardboard Fan (DIY Stardance Project)
A fan made with everyday household items such as cardboard and alunimum foil, (Except the motor).

### Demo Video: https://www.youtube.com/shorts/hPXkDbkn4BU

### AI Usage declaration:
None

## Features/Components/Stuff
- A cardbard battery holder that holds two AA batteries
- A rubber band closure for the baterry holder to prevent them from falling off
- A door that opens up into the battery holder to swap batteries
- A rubber band anchor to keep the door closed.
- A switch that functions like a push down to turn on kind of switch
- A rubber band anchor to kep the switch held down.
- A big square base
- A plastic bottle propellor
- Decoration with colored paper

## How it works
1. Put in the batteries
2. Lock the batteries in with the rubber band
3. Close the door in the upright structure
4. Lock the door with the rubber band
5. Use the switch how you want to to control the fan
